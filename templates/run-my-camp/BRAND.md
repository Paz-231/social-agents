# Run My Camp — Social Brand Brief

## Positioning
The operating platform for modern campsites.
Core message: Run your camp. Not your paperwork.

## Audience
Owners and operators of campsites and camper pitches in Austria and Germany, especially independent operators dealing with fragmented booking, payment, registration and access workflows.

## Customer problem
Too many manual steps and disconnected tools between website booking, payment, guest registration, pitch allocation, barrier access and checkout.

## Product story
Run My Camp connects the guest journey end-to-end: website/direct booking → payment → registration → arrival/access → stay → checkout.

## Proof
Campbert is the operational reference environment and has been used in real campsite operations.

## Content principles
- Show operational reality, not generic AI marketing.
- Explain concrete workflows and saved manual steps.
- Do not invent customer numbers, savings, integrations or testimonials.
- Prefer demonstrations, process comparisons and operator education.
- Product claims must be supported by the current product.
- German is the default public language.

## Content mix
Problem, education, product demo, operational insight, social proof, behind the scenes, founder/operator perspective.

## CTA style
Low-friction discussion and demo CTAs. Avoid artificial urgency.
